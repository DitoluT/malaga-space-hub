El ADCS (Attitude Determination and Control System) usa sensores (giroscopios, magnetómetros, sensores solares) y actuadores (magnetorquers) para orientar el CubeSat.

---LANG-EN---

The ADCS (Attitude Determination and Control System) uses sensors (gyros, magnetometers, sun sensors) and actuators (magnetorquers) to orient the CubeSat.
